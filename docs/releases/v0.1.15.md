# Gather v0.1.15

This one is about lag. On a small computer, Gather could freeze for several seconds when you
switched between areas, when you opened it, and while it was reading files, and the more files
it had read, the worse it got. Everything still runs on your machine.

## What changed

- **Switching areas, opening Gather and reading files no longer freeze.** Gather keeps a list
  of names it thinks are the same thing (for example "Postgres" and "PostgreSQL"), and asks for
  it every time you change area, when it opens, and every 15 to 20 seconds on Overview and
  Entities. Building that list compared every name with every other name, one pair at a time,
  and while it did, nothing else in Gather could answer, not even the check that says it is
  running. Two requests at once were enough to stop a two-core computer. Names are now prepared
  once and most pairs are ruled out by their size before anything is compared, and the work no
  longer holds up the rest of Gather. The suggestions are the same ones as before.
  On a test library of about 1,500 names, on two processor cores with the app's own work
  slowed four times to stand in for a slow computer: opening Gather went from 13.6 seconds to
  1.6, moving between areas from 6 to 38 seconds to about 1 second, and moving between areas
  while 400 files were being read from 0.8 seconds (2.4 at the slowest tenth) to 0.02 (0.5).
  The cost used to grow with the square of the number of names, so it was worst for people who
  had read the most.

- **The Graph opens without holding up everything else.** Gather used to draw every step of
  the graph settling into place. On computers without graphics acceleration drawing is slow
  (the same test showed about 6.5 seconds of the page being fully busy, and about 27 seconds
  on the slowed-down computer). The graph now settles before it is drawn, then appears. It
  takes about 1 second to open, or about 2.4 on the slowed-down computer, and the picture is
  identical. Changing what the graph shows still moves things gently.

## Not changed

Nothing in your library is touched: no files are re-read and nothing is removed. These were
measured on Linux. If Gather still feels slow on Windows or on a computer with a slow disk,
send `daemon.log` and `postgres.log` from the logs folder (`%APPDATA%\dev.gather.desktop\logs`
on Windows) and roughly how many files Gather has read.
