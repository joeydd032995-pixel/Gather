# Gather v0.1.12

A follow-up to v0.1.11, driven by real review trays that were full of noise. Everything
still runs on your machine.

## What changed

- **Source-code files are no longer read for statements.** Files such as `.rs`, `.ts`,
  `.py` and `.go` are still stored, searchable and listed in the Library, but their
  comments and doc strings are not turned into "what Gather learned". They produced most of
  the junk in the review tray (`// a brace anywhere`, `something)`, `every task`). Docs,
  READMEs, Markdown, PDFs, Word files and chats are read as before. To turn it back on, set
  `GATHER_EXTRACT_SOURCE_CODE=true`.
- **Far fewer pointless "Possible duplicate" items.** Names must now look like names before
  Gather asks whether two are the same: code literals (`null`), filler (`Everything else`),
  fragments (`every task`, `Replaying a run`), strings with stray quotes or unbalanced
  brackets, and operators are left out, and a name is never paired with its own opposite
  (`Zero` and `Non-zero`).
- **Your existing review tray cleans itself up.** On the first background pass after you
  update, Gather closes open items about pairs that are not names, and items whose two
  entities were learned only from source-code files. Nothing is deleted: they are
  dismissed, and the stored entities stay.
- **The "What Gather found" view scrolls.** The list under Explore was cut off at the
  window edge in v0.1.11.

## Not changed

Statements already learned from source-code files before this update remain in your
library; only new reads skip them.
