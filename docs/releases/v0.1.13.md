# Gather v0.1.13

Where v0.1.12 was about reading *less* junk, this one is about reading *more* of what you
actually wrote. Everything still runs on your machine.

## What changed

- **Gather understands more ways of saying the same thing.** It used to recognise a narrow
  set of sentence shapes, so ordinary phrasing slipped past unread. Now it also catches:
  - decisions worded as "we **settled on** Postgres", "we **committed to** Postgres" and
    "I **switched from** MySQL **to** Postgres", not just "we decided on";
  - what you run and where you keep things — "I **run** the daemon on a Hetzner box",
    "I **keep** backups on an external drive", and the same for *host*, *store* and
    *deploy*;
  - sentences that open with a filler word. "Basically I use Postgres" and "Yeah, we
    settled on Hetzner" were invisible before, because the opener hid the sentence
    underneath it.

  On the test corpus this took coverage from **54% to 95%** of the statements a human
  judged worth keeping, with no loss of precision.

- **Rules you hold yourself to are recorded.** "I won't use Google Analytics", "I must not
  store customer data in the US", "I never commit secrets to the repository" are kept as
  preferences. They deliberately create **no connection in the graph**: Gather will never
  come away believing you *do* the thing you just ruled out.

- **A hedge is never treated as filler.** "Maybe I use Postgres" and "Probably I use
  Postgres" are left exactly as written. Dropping the hedge would turn something you were
  unsure about into a stored fact, so Gather doesn't.

- **A fixed false positive.** "Honestly, the budget is $40,000" used to be filed under a
  subject called *"Honestly, the budget"*. It is now filed under *budget*.

- **Stricter checks on what the local model writes** (only relevant if you have Ollama
  turned on). A statement the model writes now has to be a statement — not a heading, a
  fragment or half a sentence. The prompt also now tells the model to preserve your
  hedging rather than resolve it away.

- **Security: nine advisories closed.** Two libraries Gather depends on indirectly,
  `rustls` and `quinn-proto`, were updated out of nine published advisories, one rated
  high. Plus routine updates to tokio, serde_json, uuid, libc and six desktop packages.

## Not changed

The improved reading applies to files read **after** you update. Statements already in your
library are left as they are; nothing is re-read automatically, and nothing is removed.
